Dwell magazine feature by Mandi Keighran on the Dakota Mountain Residence designed by Low Design Office. The article examines an innovative co-housing solution for a retired Texas couple and their family, demonstrating how thoughtful design can deliver luxury living within a modest budget.

Key design innovations highlighted:
- Two interlocking yet independent residences sharing a single-story layout
- Double roof system exploiting prevailing winds for passive cooling and supporting solar panels
- 33,000-gallon rainwater harvesting cistern for water independence
- Outer wood slat wrapper managing Texas heat while providing visual warmth
- Strategic use of reclaimed timber and standard finishes for cost efficiency

The project achieved approximately $162 per square foot, demonstrating LowDO's philosophy of delivering high-end design through efficient and effective material use and thoughtful environmental integration.
