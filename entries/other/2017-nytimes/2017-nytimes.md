A New York Times feature on Nana Oforiatta-Ayim's ambitious Cultural Encyclopedia project, which aims to map and archive African arts and culture across all 54 African countries.

The article profiles Oforiatta-Ayim's vision to counter the "backward narrative" often told about Africa by creating an open-source internet platform documenting the continent's rich histories, innovations, and cultural knowledge. Starting with Ghana, the encyclopedia will eventually be published in 54 volumes, one for each country.

The Kiosk Museum—featured in the article—was showcased at the Chale Wote Festival in 2016 in Accra's Jamestown district, demonstrating LowDO's contributions to African cultural documentation and preservation efforts.
