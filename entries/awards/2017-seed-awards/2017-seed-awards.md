Low Design Office was recognized with the 2017 SEED Award for the Agbogbloshie Makerspace Platform (AMP) in Accra, Ghana. The project represents a replicable, transportable, self-contained maker space workshop addressing e-waste recycling and economic opportunity. The jury praised it as "an elegant response to a complexity of issues," highlighting its potential as a replicable model with significant social, economic, and environmental impact globally.
