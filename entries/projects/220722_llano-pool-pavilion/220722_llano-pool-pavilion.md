Concept design for a pool and restaurant-bar-retreat immersed in Texas hill country.

Water signals a journey, a transformative experience, where earth and sky connect. Awareness of the body and physical world are heightened, with attention and energy focused on the natural surroundings. This celebration of nature offers discovery and reverence in the heart of a wild landscape.
