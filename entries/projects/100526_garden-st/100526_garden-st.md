This house weaves green building and contemporary design into the context of its East Austin neighborhood -- on a tight budget. It was conceived as a framing system for the lives of those within, using a variety of implicit framing devices at different scales to create subtly distinct moments that can be experienced separately or simultaneously.

The house holds and displays records of the lives within it through a continuous shelving system that simultaneously conceals ductwork, creates a guardrail, and shapes larger spaces into zones for living. In this way the house serves as an archive, enriching one’s experience of everyday life and its intimate moments with personalized framed settings.

Because one is always moving through these not-completely deﬁned spaces, one’s understanding of the house is layered with a variety of impressions that may evolve from day to day and throughout the years spent here. This is heightened by the fact that the enclosed space of the home has in fact evolved over the past decade, the architecture itself growing and adapting along with the family who lives there.

2012 Phase 1 [main house, design and construction by LowDO]
2018 Phase 2 [shed enclosure, design and construction by LowDO]
2024 Phase 3 [basement addition, design and construction by LowDO]
