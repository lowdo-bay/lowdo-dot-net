Texas Architect Magazine feature by Cameron Klepac, Assoc. AIA, examining the Wolf Creek Ranch Residence designed by Low Design Office near Burnet, Texas. The article explores the home's sustainable design principles including rainwater collection systems, passive cooling strategies, and integration with the rocky hillside landscape.

Key design elements highlighted:
- Steel and yellow pine construction cantilevered over rocky terrain
- 30,000-gallon annual rainwater collection system
- Passive cooling through natural ventilation and central void
- Perforated steel exterior balancing shelter and views
- Nearly half the structure as unconditioned porch space
- Modular design allowing future expansion

The feature emphasizes LowDO's design philosophy prioritizing economical construction with off-the-shelf materials and thoughtful integration into the landscape.
