Dwell magazine feature on the Wolf Creek Ranch Residence by Low Design Office, a steel-clad home near Burnet, Texas in the Hill Country. The article highlights LowDO's use of an industrial material palette—stained cedar, corrugated metal, and Cor-Ten steel—to create a durable, open home on 23 acres near Lake Buchanan.

Key design features highlighted:
- Steel pier and beam foundation allowing the structure to sit lightly on the land
- Two-level rectangular layout with primary living spaces upstairs and guest suite below
- Deep roof overhangs for passive cooling in the Texas climate
- Integrated rainwater harvesting system with a 29,175-gallon cistern (2,510 gallons per inch of rain)
- Potting room foyer and carport with sliding corrugated door
